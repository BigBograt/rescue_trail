# Billy's Rescue Trail

A browser game for kids: help injured animals, pick up litter and keep the planet healthy across four worlds — the Stone Age (history), Land (maths), Under the Sea (spelling) and Sky Rescue (science). Two question levels: ages 6–7 and 8–10.

Game idea and drawings by our chief game designer, Billy, age 7.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or play online at https://bigbograt.github.io/rescue_trail/

No build step or dependencies — it's a single HTML file. Progress is saved in your browser's local storage.
